# KIRITSU mark & novel hanja attribution

The **規律** (and hidden **起立**) outlines in `src/ui/kanji.ts` and
`public/icons/icon.svg`, and the **hanja in the KIRITSU novel PDF**, are all
rendered from **Yuji Boku**, a brush-regular Japanese typeface.

- Font: **Yuji Boku**
- Source: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Yuji+Boku> ·
  <https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/yujiboku>
- License: **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (OFL)
  <https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/>
- Title outlines, the KIRITSU hanko icon and the 15-glyph novel subset are
  distributed under the OFL. The application code remains MIT.

Yuji Boku is a hand-brushed regular script in the Ouyang-Xun (欧陽詢) brush
lineage: thinner and less geometric than a black Mincho, with visible brush
entry/exit shapes. It is the face KIRITSU uses as its "구양순체" identity.
Strictly proprietary 欧体/Ouyang-Xun typesetting fonts (e.g. commercial
foundry families) are not bundled; Yuji Boku is the closest free face that
matches the mark.

## Novel hanja subset (2026-11-15)

The novel manuscript (`stories/kiritsu/work/*.md`) uses exactly **15 hanja**:
`人功唯審律憑抗月監立能規起零霊`. A 15-glyph subset is committed at
`stories/kiritsu/fonts/YujiBoku-KIRITSU-subset.ttf` (**~15.7 KB**, OFL) and is
wired into the PDF build with a CJK `unicode-range`, so those glyphs render in
the same brush style as the title while the Korean body stays NanumGothic.

- Regenerate: `cd stories/kiritsu && PYTHONPATH=<pikepdf deps> python3 subset-font.py`
  (downloads the 8.5MB full font locally; `fonts/*` is git-ignored, the subset
  is the only tracked font).
- If the manuscript gains new hanja, add them to `HANJA` in `subset-font.py`,
  rerun it and rebuild the PDF; the full Yuji Boku font is not committed.

## FONT LOCKED (2026-11-15)

The kanji typeface for every KIRITSU surface — title intro, novel PDF, boot-log
律, future hanja — is **Yuji Boku Light cut** (Yuji Boku Regular eroded 8 font
units, OFL). This is final; do not reintroduce other kanji fonts. The title
glyph is generated from this cut by `tools/gen-title-svg.py`, and the intro's
20 stroke territories are hand-traced by the user and synthesized by `kanji-trace/zones.py`; `tools/gen-title-strokes.py` is retired history.

## Final choice (2026-11-15)

After comparing free alternatives (Yuji family, LXGW WenKai, Shippori Mincho B1,
Kaisei Decol/HarunoUmi, Hina Mincho, New Tegomin, Zen Old Mincho, Yusei Magic,
Reggae/RocknRoll/Rampart One) and custom rough-cut experiments, **A — Yuji Boku
(original) is the project typeface.** It is the only face that keeps the
Ouyang-style brush flavour the title was designed around; the others either
lost the brush (Mincho) or were too display-heavy.

All rendered hanja now use it, in a **light cut** of the same font:

- On user request the title/intro, the novel PDF and the boot-log glyph use an
  **outline-eroded (8 font units inward) Light cut** of Yuji Boku, generated by
  `stories/kiritsu/subset-font.py` (skia-pathops). It is not a different face —
  the same brush shapes, slightly thinner.
- title / hanko: `public/kanji/ou-guilv-tight.svg` is now **generated directly
  from the Light subset** by `tools/gen-title-svg.py` (uniform scale; no traced
  or per-glyph-tightened variant)
- title boot-log `리츠[律]`: a **single-glyph 律 Light subset** is inlined in
  `src/style.css` as `KIRI_KANJI`
- novel PDF: 15-hanja Light subset `fonts/YujiBoku-KIRITSU-subset.ttf`

`src/ui/ou-kanji.ts` (Noto Serif JP outlines, unused) was deleted so no
non-Yuji hanja asset remains in the game bundle.

If the mark is replaced, remove these paths and this attribution.

## KanjiVG stroke order and medians (v9 title animation)

The title intro's **stroke order** (規 11 strokes, 律 9) and the **centreline
medians** used by the animation are derived from **KanjiVG** (Copyright Ulrich
Apel, CC BY-SA 3.0, <https://kanjivg.tagaini.net>). The 20 filled territories
are hand-drawn by the user as cuts + stroke lines and synthesized by
`kanji-trace/zones.py`; no KanjiVG outline or Arphic/Hanzi Writer shape data is
shipped. Data: `src/ui/title-stroke-paths.ts`; current spec/verification:
`docs/TITLE_KANJI.md` §11–§14.
